Gold, the Swiss Franc and 3M Rate Differentials

An initial study of gold, Swiss-franc strength and the short-rate advantage between the United States and Switzerland.

Market prices: Yahoo Finance Rates: Investing-derived daily database Common sample: March 2011-September 2026 Research code
Research question. Do gold and the Swiss franc express a common defensive or dollar-debasement factor, and does the US-Swiss 3M yield differential help explain when their relationship strengthens or breaks?
Gold Yahoo GC=F, a stitched nearby COMEX gold-futures quote, normalized to 100 at the common starting date.
Swiss franc Yahoo CHF=X is USD/CHF. Its chart axis is inverted, so an upward red line represents a stronger franc. The green line is an idealized long-CHF futures investment after deducting the US-Swiss carry cost.
3M differential US 3M yield minus Swiss 3M yield, in percentage points, smoothed with a 7-observation rolling mean. A positive value means the US offers more carry.

Initial comparison

The top panel deliberately mirrors the earlier TLT-USO chart: gold is on the left axis and USD/CHF is on an inverted right axis. The lower panel keeps the interest-rate differential separate because it is a yield spread, not a price.

First observations

  • Gold and CHF strength share some broad defensive episodes, but their paths are not equivalent.
  • The US 3M yield has generally remained above the Swiss 3M yield, especially from 2022 onward.
  • Gold rose strongly alongside a wide positive US-Swiss carry differential, so the spread alone cannot explain gold's level.
  • The carry-adjusted CHF investment can materially lag the visual inverted USD/CHF line when the US rate advantage is large.
  • The relationship appears regime-dependent and should be tested on changes or returns, not inferred from two trending price levels.
Important convention: a falling raw USD/CHF quote means CHF appreciation. The inverted axis makes that appreciation move upward visually; it does not transform the underlying cached data.

Data construction

Rates come from market_dashboard.bond_yield_history_daily. Switzerland_3M begins on 24 March 2011. The historical US series US_3M ends on 14 September 2026 and is continued with United States_3M from 15 September 2026. Both source tables and market prices are saved locally before plotting.

Long-CHF futures proxy

The daily proxy return is the percentage change in 1 / USDCHF minus the previous observation's annualized US 3M - Swiss 3M differential divided by 252. The spread uses the same 7-observation mean as the lower panel. This approximates the carry paid by a USD-funded long-CHF position.

Approximation: this is not a contract-level futures backtest. It excludes futures basis, expiry rolls, margin collateral return, commissions and execution slippage.
