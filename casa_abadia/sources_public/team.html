Team and structure

Who does what on this project and how the contracts are organised. Status as of 8 Nov 2026: the construction contract is in preparation. The contract, once signed, is the binding text; this page is a summary.

Contract chain

OwnersNatasha Santric and Sébastien Maillard
→
BacofraMain contractor (empreiteiro) for the whole works
→
Wood HomeNominated subcontractor for the timber structure
  • Bacofra is the single contractor of record: foundations, timber structure, site equipment and services, coordination.
  • Wood Home is the subcontractor nominated by the owners, under an agreement negotiated by the owners and signed by Bacofra as buyer.
  • Everything is invoiced by Bacofra to the owners. Payments go from the owners to Bacofra and from Bacofra to Wood Home.

Working rules already agreed

  • Factory made openings: duct and service openings through structural panels are made in the factory, not cut on site.
  • Anything that goes through or under the slab is fixed, capped, photographed and recorded before the pour.
  • Bacofra sends a short monthly progress note to Paulo (OTK).
